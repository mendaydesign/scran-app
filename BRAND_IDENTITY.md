# SCRAN — Brand Identity Reference

> This document is written for a marketing/creative agent. It covers the full brand identity of SCRAN: name, personality, visual language, colour system, typography, and tone of voice. Use this as the authoritative brand reference when producing any marketing material.

---

## 1. The Brand in One Sentence

**SCRAN is the recipe app that makes finding what to cook feel like scrolling through a magazine, not a spreadsheet.**

---

## 2. Product Name

**SCRAN** — all caps in logotype contexts.

"Scran" is British and Irish slang for food. It's casual, punchy, and has immediate cultural resonance for the target audience. It communicates that this is not a po-faced health app — it's about the pleasure of food.

---

## 3. Who It's For

Home cooks — specifically people who:
- Want to cook interesting meals but struggle to decide what to make
- Are bored of their usual rotation
- Enjoy the discovery aspect of browsing but find traditional recipe sites overwhelming
- Range from beginner to confident home cook

The tone should always feel like advice from a friend who loves food, not instruction from a professional chef.

---

## 4. Brand Personality

| Trait | What it means in practice |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Confident** | Bold visual choices, strong typography, no apologies |
| **Editorial** | Feels like a premium food magazine, not a utility app |
| **Playful** | The swipe mechanic is fun by design — lean into that |
| **Direct** | Short copy, strong verbs, no waffle |
| **Modern** | Fashion-forward, digitally native, not retro or rustic |

SCRAN is **not** wholesome, not pastel, not "clean eating" wellness culture. It's energetic, graphic, and a little bit loud.

---

## 5. Visual Identity

### Logo
- A bold lime-yellow **"S"** lettermark on a deep forest green rounded square
- The "S" is set in Clash Grotesk Bold — heavy, confident, oversized within the frame
- The rounded square uses large corner radius — consistent with the app's "pill and poster" shape language
- **Primary logo colours:** Background `#04492B` (forest green), lettermark `#D5FB2A` (lime)

### Shape Language — "Pill and Poster"
Everything in SCRAN uses exaggerated rounded corners. This is a core visual signature:
- Cards, containers, and buttons all use oversized border radius (`32px` or fully rounded pills)
- The roundness communicates approachability and a modern, digitally-native feel
- Avoid sharp corners in any marketing material — they break the brand language

### The Graphic Editorial Direction
SCRAN's visual direction is called **"The Graphic Editorial."** Key principles:
- Typography is used as a structural, visual element — headlines should feel almost too large
- High-contrast colour blocking — deep green against lime, or green against warm off-white
- Breathing room is essential — the contrast needs space to land
- Asymmetry and image bleed are encouraged over centred, "safe" layouts
- Imagery should feel like editorial food photography — real, textured, appetising

## 6. Colour System

These are the exact brand colours. Use them precisely.

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Forest Green (Primary)** | `#04492B` | Main brand colour — backgrounds, buttons, logo, headers |
| **Lime (Accent)** | `#D5FB2A` | Primary CTA buttons, active states, logo lettermark, highlights |
| **Warm Off-White (Background)** | `#FFFCF6` | App canvas, light backgrounds in marketing materials |
| **Surface** | `#F6F3ED` | Secondary backgrounds, card fills |
| **Surface High** | `#F0EEE6` | Nested card backgrounds |
| **Vivid Pink (Secondary)** | `#B0255A` | Secondary accent — "nope" interactions, energy accents |
| **Text Primary** | `#383834` | All body copy — warm near-black, never pure black |
| **Text Secondary** | `#66635D` | Captions, metadata |

### Key Colour Pairings
- **Hero combination:** Forest Green `#04492B` + Lime `#D5FB2A` — the signature SCRAN pairing
- **Light combination:** Warm Off-White `#FFFCF6` + Forest Green `#04492B`
- **Accent splash:** Pink `#B0255A` used sparingly to add energy, never as a primary background

### What to Avoid
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) — always use the warm off-white instead
- Pure black (`#000000`) — use `#383834` for text
- Pastels, muted tones, or "clean eating" greens (bright, cool greens) — SCRAN's green is deep and rich, not fresh and minty

## 7. Typography

SCRAN uses two typefaces exclusively.

### Clash Grotesk Bold — The Display Voice
- Used for: all headings, titles, CTA button labels, card titles, feature names
- Character: heavy, grotesque-inspired, confident, fashion-forward
- Feels "too big for its container" — this is intentional and desirable
- In marketing: use tight tracking for large display sizes

### Neue Haas Display Medium — The Body Voice
- Used for: all body copy, captions, descriptions, metadata
- Character: clean, geometric, balanced, legible
- Provides contrast against the loud headlines — clarity without competing

### Typography Rules for Marketing
- Headlines should dominate — if the headline doesn't feel slightly oversized, make it bigger
- Never use light or thin weights — SCRAN is bold
- All-caps is used selectively for metadata and labels (e.g. badge text on cards) — don't overuse it in long copy
- Line height on headlines: 1.1–1.2× — tight, editorial

---

## 8. Photography & Imagery Direction

When selecting or commissioning food photography for SCRAN:
- **Real, textured, honest food** — not overly styled or sterile studio shots
- **Warm lighting** — consistent with the warm off-white palette
- **Hero shots** — food filling the frame, shot close, often from above or 45 degrees
- **No hands unless intentional** — the food is the subject
- Avoid white plate on white background — needs contrast and texture

---

## 9. Tone of Voice

| Situation | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| App taglines | "Swipe. Cook. Eat." | "Discover your next culinary adventure" |
| Feature descriptions | "Add what's in your kitchen. We'll find the recipes." | "Utilise our intelligent pantry matching system" |
| CTAs | "Get Swiping" / "Let's Cook" | "Get Started" / "Begin Your Journey" |
| Error states | "Nothing here yet." | "No items found in this category at this time." |

**Voice principles:**
- Short sentences. Strong verbs.
- British English spelling (colour, flavour, practise)
- Casual but never sloppy — confident, not careless
- Food enthusiasm is genuine, not performative

---

## 10. App Icon

- Forest green rounded square (`#04492B` background)
- Lime `#D5FB2A` bold "S" centred within the frame
- Heavy weight, clean geometry
- No gradients, no additional elements
- Consistent with the logo — the icon IS the logo at small size
